# The Water Cycle

The water cycle describes how water moves through Earth's atmosphere,
land, and oceans.

- **Evaporation**: Heat from the sun turns liquid water in oceans, lakes,
  and rivers into water vapor, which rises into the atmosphere.
- **Transpiration**: Plants release water vapor into the air through
  their leaves.
- **Condensation**: As water vapor rises and cools, it condenses into
  tiny droplets, forming clouds.
- **Precipitation**: When cloud droplets combine and grow heavy enough,
  they fall back to Earth as rain, snow, sleet, or hail.
- **Collection**: Precipitation collects in oceans, lakes, and rivers,
  or soaks into the ground as groundwater, and the cycle begins again.

The sun is the primary energy source that drives the entire cycle by
powering evaporation. Gravity drives precipitation and the flow of
water back toward the oceans.
